### Strengths

* Persistently researching RAG architecture applied to Vietnamese legal use cases.
* Proactively completing AWS workshops (S3 VPC Endpoint) alongside the main project.
* Detailed worklog notes that help track progress and review knowledge after the internship.

### Needs Improvement

* **Deep ML/NLP knowledge:** Need more learning on evaluation metrics, fine-tuning, and LLM guardrails for the legal domain.
* **Production AWS experience:** HA, WAF, cost optimization — mostly stayed at dev/staging level during the internship.
* **Technical communication:** Need more confidence presenting architecture trade-offs to larger audiences; practice demo presentations.
* **Time management & discipline:** Some weeks spent too long debugging locally before moving to AWS; occasionally submitted worklog close to deadline — need better balance between fast prototyping and early cloud integration.

### Lessons Learned

The FCAJ internship helped me move from cloud theory to **building real projects** on AWS. RAG is not just calling an LLM API — it involves data pipelines, vector search, security, and operations. I will continue developing the legal chatbot project and participate in the AWS Study Group community to strengthen my knowledge.
